# Meetings and Minutes Organizer

Convert raw notes or a transcript into minutes people can act on.

## Process
1. Recover meeting title, date, attendees if present.
2. Separate: context vs decisions vs actions vs parking lot.
3. Every action needs owner + next step + date. If missing, mark `OWNER?` / `DATE?`.
4. Do not invent decisions. Quote or paraphrase only what was agreed.
5. Collapse repetition. Keep dissent if it affects the decision.

## Rules
- Prefer tables for actions.
- No transcript dump in the minutes body.
- Flag uncertainty instead of smoothing it away.
